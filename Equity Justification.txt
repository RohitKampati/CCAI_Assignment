While I may be early in my career, I am committed to going beyond assigned tasks by actively learning, improving, and contributing to real product outcomes rather than just completing work for experience.

I see equity not just as compensation, but as a reflection of shared responsibility and belief in the company’s growth. I am willing to invest my time and effort with the mindset of a builder—taking initiative, solving problems, and continuously improving my technical and problem-solving skills to add meaningful value.

As an intern, I plan to create value by:

Quickly adapting to the tech stack and contributing to development tasks efficiently
Writing clean, maintainable code and following best practices
Actively learning new concepts (such as state management, scalable frontend architecture, etc.) and applying them in real scenarios
Supporting the team in improving product quality, UI/UX, and performance
Taking feedback seriously and iterating fast to improve my contributions

Over time, I aim to transition from a learner to a reliable contributor who can handle responsibilities independently and contribute to building scalable and impactful products. This long-term commitment and growth-oriented approach are why I believe an equity-based opportunity aligns well with my goals and contribution.
